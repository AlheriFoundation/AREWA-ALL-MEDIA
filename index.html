GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Does Arewa All Media download from every platform?

No. We recognize the six listed platforms, but direct download options appear only when an authorized provider integration permits them. We never bypass logins, DRM, or platform restrictions.

Do you store the links I submit?

The frontend does not store submitted URLs. A production deployment should also configure privacy-conscious, redacted request logging and short server timeouts.

Can I download private content?

No. Private, restricted, or access-controlled content is not supported.
