PRIVACY

Your links deserve care.

This starter policy explains the intended data handling for Arewa All Media. Have counsel review it before launch.

What we process

When you submit a URL, the service may process it briefly to validate its format and recognize its host. Do not submit private or sensitive URLs. The frontend does not save submitted links in local storage.

What we do not do

We do not sell submitted URLs or use them for advertising profiles. Production logging should be configured to avoid storing full submitted URLs, tokens, or provider response bodies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact address published by the site operator.
